Funded Account Payout Consistency

Consistency is checked when reviewing a funded account's payout eligibility. It is separate from the evaluation pass/fail rules.

Consistency Formula

Largest Profitable Day ÷ Total Net Profit × 100

The percentage of total net profit earned on the trader's largest profitable day must not exceed the account's limit.
