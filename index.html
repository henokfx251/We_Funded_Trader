Need To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about WFT programs, trading rules, pricing and discounts.

Which funding programs does WFT offer?

WFT offers seven programs: 2-Step Standard, 2-Step Pro, 1-Step Standard, 1-Step Pro, Instant Standard, Instant Plus and Instant Pro. Each program has its own rules and price.

What are the profit targets?

2-Step Standard: 10% in Phase 1 and 5% in Phase 2. 2-Step Pro: 6% in each phase. 1-Step Standard: 10%. 1-Step Pro: 6%. Instant programs do not have an evaluation target.

How do the drawdown rules work?

Each program has its own maximum and daily loss limits. Static limits use the stated fixed reference. 1-Step Pro and Instant Pro use equity-based rules, with maximum loss trailing the highest recorded equity. Floating losses count where equity is used.

When does daily drawdown reset?

2-Step Standard uses a rolling 24-hour reset from the account start time. Programs marked Daily Reset use the broker server day. Instant Standard has no daily drawdown limit.

What is the minimum profitable-day requirement?

All seven WFT programs require at least 3 profitable days. Each counted day must reach at least 0.25% profit.

Can I restart an Instant Standard account?

No. Instant Standard is closed-positions-only and does not offer a restart.

Which account sizes are available?

$2,500, $5,000, $10,000, $25,000, $50,000, $100,000, $200,000 and $300,000. $300,000 is the highest available account size.

How do WFT discount codes work?

NEW50 gives eligible first-time customers 50% off their first order. WFT20 gives an ongoing 20% discount and WFT10 gives an ongoing 10% discount. Only one coupon code can be applied to an order. NEW50 eligibility is checked against your order history.

Which consistency rule applies to each account?

All Instant Funding accounts (Instant Standard, Instant Plus and Instant Pro) use a 30% consistency rule. All 1-Step funded accounts (1-Step Standard and 1-Step Pro) use a 50% consistency rule. 2-Step Standard and 2-Step Pro have no consistency rule.

What is the trader profit split and payout schedule?

1-Step Standard and Pro: 80% trader / 20% WFT. 2-Step Standard and Pro: 85% trader / 15% WFT. Instant Standard, Plus and Pro: 75% trader / 25% WFT. Payouts start after 14 days and then every 14 days, with a $50 minimum and 1–3 business day processing.

Does the WFT TradingView Terminal execute trades?

No. The WFT TradingView Terminal is for chart analysis only. It does not execute broker trades.
