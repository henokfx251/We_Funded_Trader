Challenge Rules

1-Step Evaluation: 10% profit target, 3% daily drawdown, 5% maximum drawdown, at least 5 profitable days and 0.5% minimum profit per profitable day. Drawdown uses live equity, including floating losses on open trades.

Instant Funding: No evaluation phase, 5% maximum drawdown and no daily limit. Drawdown uses live equity; closed trades count toward profit and consistency calculations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about your WFT account.

When can I request a payout?

First payout eligibility begins 14 days after the account start date. Subsequent payouts are available every 14 days after the previous completed payout, subject to eligibility checks.

Which payout networks are supported?

USDT TRC20 and BEP20 are supported. Always verify your wallet network before submitting.

How long does payout processing take?

Normally 1–3 business days after approval.

When is my certificate issued?

After your challenge pass request is reviewed and approved by WFT Admin.
