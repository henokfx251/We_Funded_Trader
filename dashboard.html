Support & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about your account.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the 1-Step Evaluation rules?

Profit target: 10%. Daily drawdown: 3%. Maximum drawdown: 5%. Minimum profitable day: 0.5%. You must achieve 5 profitable days to complete the evaluation.

What is the 1-Step Funded profit split?

For a 1-Step Funded account, the trader receives 80% of eligible profits and the company receives 20%.

What is the 1-Step payout consistency rule?

The consistency limit is 50%. The maximum withdrawal is 6% and the minimum withdrawal is 2%.

What are the Instant Funding rules?

Instant Funding has no daily drawdown. Maximum drawdown is 5%, measured on closed positions only.

What is the Instant Funding profit split?

The trader receives 70% of eligible profits and the company receives 30%.

What is the Instant Funding payout rule?

The consistency limit is 30%. The minimum withdrawal is 1% and the maximum withdrawal is 4%.

When can I request my first payout?

The first payout is available after 14 days. After that, payouts are scheduled every 14 days, subject to eligibility and review.

How long does payout processing take?

Approved payout processing normally takes 1–3 business days.

Which payout networks are supported?

USDT TRC20 and USDT BEP20 are the supported payout networks. Always provide the correct wallet address for the selected network.

What are the Reward Account rules?

A reward account redeemed with points requires a $1 activation fee. It allows one withdrawal only, up to a maximum of 3%.

Can I continue after receiving a Reward Account payout?

After receiving the reward account payout, a restart fee applies. You can also continue by paying the full standard trading account fee in installments, subject to the available arrangement. For example, a $10,000 account has a standard fee of $40.

How does the Affiliate Program work?

Share your personal referral link. Referral commissions and rewards are subject to the official affiliate program terms. The applicable commission rate will be shown when configured.

How do I submit KYC?

Open KYC Verification, enter your legal name and country, select your document type, and upload the front side of your ID. JPG, PNG and PDF files up to 10 MB are accepted.

How can I contact support?

For account, payment, KYC or payout assistance, email support@wefundedtrader.com.
