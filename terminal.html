ICT Analysis Guide

Follow the top-down analysis process before making a trading decision.

1. Higher Timeframe Bias

Analyze Weekly and Daily structure.

  • Identify bullish or bearish trend.
  • Mark previous highs and lows.
  • Identify major liquidity pools.
  • Mark Premium and Discount zones.

2. Market Structure

Use 4H and 1H for confirmation.

  • Identify Order Blocks.
  • Mark Fair Value Gaps.
  • Watch for liquidity sweeps.
  • Confirm BOS or MSS.

3. Entry Confirmation

Use 15M and 5M for execution planning.

  • Wait for liquidity sweep.
  • Look for displacement.
  • Confirm Market Structure Shift.
  • Plan entry, SL and TP.
